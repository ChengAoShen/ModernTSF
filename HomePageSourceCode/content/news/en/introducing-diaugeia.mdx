Today we are introducing **Diaugeia.AI** — transparent, open-source infrastructure for AI research.

The name comes from the Greek **διαύγεια**: clarity, lucidity, transparency. It captures what we are after — research that is reproducible by design, agentic in practice, and open at every layer.

## Why now

AI research has never been more capable, or more bottlenecked by infrastructure. Compute orchestration, reproducible environments, datasets, and evaluation absorb an enormous share of researchers' time — and the work is rebuilt, privately, in lab after lab.

We think that groundwork should be shared.

## What we stand for

- **Modern** — built on cutting-edge design and continuously updated to stay at the frontier.
- **Agentic** — tightly integrated with LLM agents to minimize the manual work humans need to do.
- **Reproducibility** — every result is traceable, re-runnable, and verifiable.
- **Open by default** — transparent, auditable, and free to build upon.

## What's next

In the coming months we will open up the platform piece by piece. Follow along here in News — Diaugeia.AI is open and built in the public interest, and we would love for you to be part of it.
